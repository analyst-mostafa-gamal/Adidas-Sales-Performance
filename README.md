## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive, end-to-end Power BI solution designed to track and analyze the sales performance of **Adidas** across multiple regions and product categories. This project transforms raw retail data into actionable business insights, focusing on revenue growth, customer behavior, and employee efficiency.  

## ⚙️ Technical Approach
- **Advanced DAX:** Implemented complex measures for **MoM (Month-over-Month)** and **YoY (Year-over-Year)** growth comparisons.
- **Interactive UI/UX:** Built a custom monochrome branded interface with a functional navigation bar and intuitive iconography.  
- **Data Modeling:** Designed a Snowflake schema to handle multiple dimensions, including Products, Customers, Geography, and Employees.  
- **Granular Reporting:** Included "Drill-Through" detail pages for deep-dive analysis into product and employee metrics.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop:** For data modeling and visualization.  
- **Power Query:** For ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes and data cleaning.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** For calculated columns and measures.

## 📊 Key Insights
- **Revenue Growth:** Achieved a total sales volume of **101K**, with a year-over-year (YoY) increase of **51K**.
- **Regional Performance:** Egypt emerged as the primary market **(46% of sales)**, with Cairo being the top-performing city.  
- **Product Analytics:** Identified "Hero Products" (Backpack, Superstar, and Stan Smith), which collectively drive the majority of revenue.
- **Human Capital:** Monitored sales representative efficiency, identifying top performers and monthly ranking fluctuations using advanced visualization techniques.

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights
1. **Overview:** High-level KPIs including Total Sales, Trends, and Regional Distribution.
2. **Customers:** Demographic breakdown and top customer identification.  
3. **Products:** Category performance and individual product sales rankings.  
4. **Employees:** Sales rep rankings, transaction counts, and efficiency metrics.
5. **Details:** Tabular data for granular performance auditing.
